What the results mean
  • No modded parts found: Parts and level pass the game-rule checks.
  • Order issue: The same parts can satisfy the other checks, but they are written in a different order than the item’s slot list. This alone does not show the item was modded. Firmware is not always last; shields can have parts after it, and a packed number block keeps its written order.
  • Modded: An impossible part combination or level. The reason tells you which rule failed.
  • Usually unobtainable: NPC, AI or vehicle equipment may be spawnable even when it is not normally available as player loot. Kept separate from modded-only exports.
  • Cannot check / verify: The result names the missing data or unverified exception. These items are kept out of the passed list.
  • Invalid serial: The code could not be read. Paste the complete code again.

This checks whether a roll is allowed. A legal roll can also be created in an editor, so serials cannot prove who made an item.
